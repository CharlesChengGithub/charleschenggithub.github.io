Cost-Based Price Floors, Soft Budgets and Capacity Adjustment in China’s Solar Photovoltaic Industry

与张金林合作·Sustainability，已录用

构建光伏组件行业的多主体模型，评估禁止低于成本销售的政策。以企业自身平均总成本为底价，会降低产能利用率和福利、延缓退出；以行业参考成本为底价能稳定价格，但使退出产能减少 75%。

摘要（英文）

Persistent overcapacity in photovoltaic (PV) manufacturing raises a question for the economic sustainability of renewable-energy supply chains: does protecting producers’ margins help or hinder capacity adjustment? We evaluate cost-based price floors with an agent-based model of China’s PV module industry that features adaptive pricing, market-share-driven investment, credit guarantees and local-government rescues. The model is driven by observed polysilicon prices and reproduces eight of ten moments from public company data within a pre-registered tolerance, although it captures only part of the 2024 price decline. Policy effects are paired comparisons across 200 random seeds. A floor at each firm’s own average total cost lowers utilisation by 5.8 percentage points and welfare by 1.1% over ten years and, in the main specification, slows exit even under hard budgets; its welfare loss grows when products are more substitutable and turns into a gain only when demand is much less price-sensitive, R&D responds strongly to profits or share pressure is very high. An industry-reference floor stabilises prices but cuts capacity exit by 75%; its 1.8% welfare gain falls to 0.5% when surviving firms are not valued as additional variety. An admission standard for new capacity raises utilisation and, while demand plateaus, welfare. Protecting margins is a poor substitute for policies that change the returns to expansion.

我国商品期货投资者锚定效应的存在性及其对市场有效性的影响

与邝雄、陈霞合作·系统科学与数学, 2022, 42(8): 2126–2141

用期货投资者日度交易数据检验出商品期货投资者的报价锚定效应，并用多主体模拟考察它对市场有效性的影响：套利者的锚定降低价格波动、提升价格发现功能；投机者的锚定虽能稳定价格，却削弱价格发现功能。

摘要

文章基于中国期货投资者日度交易数据，在排除了个体信息对锚定偏差的混淆影响后，检验出中国期货市场投资者在投资商品期货的过程中，其当期报价会对上一次报价存在显著的锚定效应。在实证检验的基础上，文章进一步构建了期货市场上存在锚定效应的投资者交易决策的数理模型，并运用多主体复杂系统模拟的方法对锚定效应和期货市场有效性之间的关系进行了模拟研究。研究发现，套利者的锚定效应能够有效降低期货市场的价格波动性同时提升期货市场对现货市场的价格发现功能；而投机者的锚定效应虽然能稳定期货市场的价格波动，但是却降低了期货市场的价格发现功能。

When Artificial Intelligence Generates and Experts Validate: Specialist Allocation and the Aggregate Substitutability of Artificial Intelligence in Research

与张金林合作·返修中，Systems

人工智能生成候选方案、专家负责验证时，整个研发过程对专家的互补性强于生成环节本身。按任务层面的证据校准，整体替代弹性为 0.95，AI 在研发中的产出弹性已进入下降阶段。

摘要（英文）

Artificial intelligence (AI) increasingly generates candidate hypotheses, molecules, and designs, but candidates become usable knowledge only after specialists validate them. We study how this division of labor shapes the substitutability between AI and specialists in research as a whole. When generation and validation share a pool of specialists, the aggregate elasticity of substitution is a weighted average of the elasticity within generation and the elasticity between the two tasks, with a weight equal to AI’s share in generation times the share of specialists in validation. Optimal staffing raises this weight as AI becomes abundant, so research is more complementary than its generation stage, and AI’s output elasticity in research rises only while the aggregate elasticity exceeds one. Gains confined to generation draw specialists into validation; sufficiently large gains in validation reverse the flow. In a dynamic general equilibrium model, the aggregate elasticity is a sufficient statistic for local aggregate dynamics, so aggregate data cannot reveal the task structure, but staffing data can. Calibrated to task-level evidence, the aggregate elasticity is 0.95, AI’s output elasticity in research is already declining, and ignoring validation overstates it by 25 percent.

Do Below-Cost Sales Bans Facilitate Algorithmic Collusion? Price Floors, Learning and Cost-Based Regulation

在审，Computational Economics

底价设在略低于竞争价格处时很少真正约束价格，Q-learning 定价算法学到的合谋加成却会提高。按实际销量分摊固定成本的完全成本底价，会引发“成本加成死亡螺旋”。

摘要（英文）

Governments are tightening rules against below-cost sales to curb destructive price wars, while firms increasingly delegate pricing to self-learning algorithms. This paper studies how price floors shape the prices that such algorithms learn. I replicate the Q-learning duopoly of Calvano et al. (2020), widen its price grid so that unregulated algorithms can price below cost, and impose floors that differ in level and in the cost definition behind them. Collusion is measured against the floor-constrained competitive benchmark. The effect depends on where the floor sits. A floor just below the competitive price rarely binds, yet it raises the collusive markup by 0.065, or 14 percent of the Nash–monopoly price gap, and the markup rises in all nine alternative economies examined. The effect arises during learning: a floor removed partway through learning still raises prices, whereas a floor imposed after learning barely moves them. Higher floors truncate punishments and crowd out the collusive markup, as repeated-game theory predicts, but the mechanical rise in the competitive benchmark keeps prices near the collusive level. When fixed costs are allocated over actual sales, a full-cost floor creates a cost-plus death spiral: a firm that loses sales faces a rising legal floor and is excluded in every session. Allocating fixed costs over normal output avoids this trap and lowers markups.

Taking Instructions at Their Word: Price Floors, Prompt Protocols and LLM Pricing Agents

在审，Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization

大模型定价主体会按字面理解指令：公布底价后，多数主体以底价开局，即使没有强制执行也予以遵守。底价此后成为最终价格还是抬价的起点，取决于所用模型；常用提示词中关于顾客最高支付意愿的表述，会使长期价格升至接近垄断水平。

摘要（英文）

Competition authorities worry that price floors, such as bans on below-cost sales, may help pricing algorithms collude. I study how pricing agents built on large language models (LLMs) respond to floors in a repeated logit duopoly, using 820 sessions of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and a replication with Qwen2.5-7B, each under a pre-analysis plan. The agents take the text of their instructions at its word. Most agents of both models open at an announced floor and obey it even when nothing enforces it, although without the floor about half (DeepSeek) or all (Qwen) of their final prices lie below it. What follows depends on the model. DeepSeek stays at the floor: about two-thirds of its final prices equal it exactly, prices rise no more than mechanical truncation implies (95% confidence interval for the excess: [−0.081, 0.000]), and agents call the floor the focal point for coordination. Qwen climbs from the floor: every market ends above it, and profits close about half the gap between the floor-constrained competitive and monopoly levels. The same literalism governs the prompt protocol. A widely used sentence telling agents that no customer would pay more than a stated amount is taken as a fact about demand: agents open at that amount, long-run prices rise to about the monopoly level, and an unrelated sentence about price display raises them further. Under that protocol the floors rarely bind. Compliance with a stated rule holds across models; whether the floor becomes the price or a launch pad does not.

When Pilots Overlap: Contamination Bias in Difference-in-Differences Evaluations of China’s City Pilot Policies

与张金林合作·返修中，Sustainability

我国城市试点政策高度重叠，单独评估某一试点的双重差分估计会混入其他试点的效应。以城市 PM2.5 校准的蒙特卡洛模拟显示，共存试点与目标试点同样有效时，目标效应被高估的中位数为 78%—139%；加入其他试点虚拟变量这一常见做法只会使偏误反向，不能消除偏误。

摘要（英文）

Pilot programmes are central to China’s environmental and low-carbon policy, and their effects are usually estimated one programme at a time with staggered difference-in-differences (DID) designs. The programmes, however, overlap. Of 297 cities in 2000–2022, 169 hosted two or more of ten national pilot programmes by 2022, and 75–95% of each programme’s treated city-years overlapped with another programme. Monte Carlo simulations calibrated to city PM2.5, with known effects for every programme, show that this overlap contaminates DID evaluations. When each co-located programme is as effective as the focal one, two-way fixed effects, event-study and heterogeneity-robust estimators overstate the focal effect by a median 78–139%. A programme with no effect is found significant in 11–22% of simulated evaluations when co-located effects are small (0.6%), and in 71–100% in a stress test that sets each to the median of three verifiable published estimates (−3.45%). Adding dummies for the other programmes, the most common robustness check, reverses the bias rather than removing it, and pretrend tests rarely detect the problem. Because the contamination is a weighted sum of the other programmes’ effects, with weights fixed by the treatment pattern, it can be computed before any outcome data are examined; we provide code and a checklist.

Who Gets Liquidated? Margin Requirements and the Reallocation of Liquidation Risk in Futures Markets: An Agent-Based Systems Analysis

与张金林合作·在审，Systems

保证金不仅影响强制平仓的规模，也决定谁被平仓。以期货公司客户数据为经验基础的多主体模型显示，下调保证金率使新增强平几乎全部落在套利者身上；上调保证金率则把强平转移给受自身杠杆约束的投机者。

摘要（英文）

Margin requirements are usually judged by how much forced liquidation they prevent. They also shape who is liquidated. Futures margins cap leverage before positions are opened and trigger margin calls afterward, binding different traders through different constraints. We model speculators and leveraged arbitrageurs interacting through margin calls and price impact. Client data from a Chinese futures brokerage show that trading directions are more concentrated than chance (LSV measure 0.0079, p < 0.001). We derive margin-call thresholds analytically and evaluate results over 100 feasible points in a 16-dimensional parameter space (35,000 paths). Cutting the margin rate from 8% to 4% is lose–lose: liquidation rises at every point, almost entirely among arbitrageurs, and mispricing widens at 95% of points. Within 100 periods, raising the rate to 20% reduces liquidation while increasing mispricing incidence and shifting liquidation to speculators bound by their own leverage limits. Exchanging the roles’ constraints reverses the shift, showing that it tracks the constraint configuration rather than the arbitrage role. At high margins, herding accompanies higher speculator leverage and greater liquidation. Margin policy should be assessed by whose leverage constraint it tightens.

Pooled Margin Accounts and Cross-Product Transmission in Futures Markets: Account Evidence and a Calibrated Agent-Based Model

在审，Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization

同一保证金账户中其他品种的亏损，会促使期货投资者削减并平掉持仓，这一反应在保证金约束未绑定的账户中同样存在。经间接推断校准的三品种多主体模型显示，合并账户所避免的强制平仓远多于其跨品种传导的强制平仓，对其他品种价格几乎没有影响。

摘要（英文）

Futures investors often trade several products from one margin account, and daily marking to market turns a loss in one product into an immediate loss of funds for all of them. Using 2019–2020 position records from a Chinese futures brokerage, I measure the mark-to-market gains and losses that an account's previous-day positions incur at the next settlement and estimate how gains and losses on other products change the account's position in a given product. Losses on other products lead investors to cut and close positions. The response is present in accounts whose margin constraint does not bind and is as strong for losses from unrelated sectors and weakly correlated products as for losses from related ones. The additional response of constrained accounts has the expected sign but is imprecise. Gains have weaker effects, confined to correlated products, and raise the probability of opening new products. I then calibrate the strength of this profit-and-loss feedback in a three-product agent-based model by indirect inference and compare pooled accounts with accounts segregated by product. Pooling cuts liquidations in the shocked product by more than it adds in other products, and leaves other products' prices essentially unchanged. The calibrated feedback produces a large but short-lived cross-product position cut with no detectable price effect, and lowers margin use in calm periods. Cross-product position cuts in the data come mainly from investors' own responses. Cross-product forced liquidations arise only through pooled funds, which on net prevent far more liquidations than they transmit.

Fleeing or flocking? Generative AI and the demand for college majors in China

工作论文

利用三个省份约 9.8 万个院校专业的高考录取分数线发现，ChatGPT 发布后，大语言模型暴露度更高的专业录取门槛相对上升。考生没有逃离受 AI 冲击的专业，反而更多地选择了这些专业。

摘要（英文）

Generative artificial intelligence can perform many tasks done by college-educated workers, and students choosing a field of study may anticipate this. This study examines how demand for college majors in China changed after the release of ChatGPT, using programme-level admission cutoffs from the national college entrance examination (gaokao) in three provinces that admit students by "major and institution". The data cover about 98,000 programmes and 338,000 programme-year observations from 2020 to 2026. Majors with different exposure to large language models are compared within the same institution and year. Contrary to the expectation that students would avoid exposed fields, the admission thresholds of more exposed majors rose relative to those of less exposed majors at the same institution: a one-standard-deviation increase in exposure is associated with a 5 per cent decline in the cutoff rank, that is, a more selective entry threshold. The pattern holds for three exposure measures, including one constructed before ChatGPT, in each province, in both STEM and other fields, and when computer science and electronics are excluded. Exposed majors were already gaining ground before 2023, but the 2023 admission round shows a discrete shift of about 2.3 per cent per standard deviation beyond the pre-existing trend. Planned enrolment in exposed majors also expanded. In the first four admission cycles after ChatGPT, there is no evidence that Chinese applicants fled majors exposed to generative AI; if anything, they moved towards them.

Generative AI Adoption and Back-Office Employment: Early Evidence from Chinese Listed Firms

工作论文

2025 年首次披露内部使用生成式 AI 的上市公司，当年财务人员和行政人员的占比与人数都没有下降。企业主要把这项技术用于生产和研发，而不是后台事务。

摘要（英文）

Accountants and clerks rank among the workers most exposed to generative artificial intelligence (AI). Do firms that adopt the technology employ fewer of them? We read the 2022–2025 annual reports of 5,706 Chinese listed firms and identify 159 non-financial firms that first disclosed using generative AI in their own operations in 2025, the year in which open-weight models made in-house deployment cheap. Because listed firms must report their workforce by function, we can compare the finance and administrative staff of these adopters with those of 2,714 firms that disclosed neither internal use nor AI products. Adoption leaves the finance-staff share unchanged: the 2025 estimate is 0.06 percentage points, and the confidence interval rules out declines larger than 2.7% of the pre-adoption mean. The number of finance employees is equally stable. The administrative-staff share and headcount also show no decline, but adopters had been expanding administrative staff faster than non-adopters before adoption, and this relative expansion stopped in 2025; if the earlier trend is extrapolated, a slowdown of up to 10% cannot be ruled out. Firms that adopted in 2023 and 2024 show no decline one to two years later. The disclosures reveal a mismatch between exposure and deployment: firms apply generative AI mainly in production and R&D, and only one of the 159 adopters explicitly applies it to finance or accounting work. In its first year, enterprise adoption of generative AI has not displaced back-office employment.

持亏是偏差还是耐心？——处置效应、交易功能与期货市场定价效率

与张金林合作·工作论文

持有亏损仓位究竟是偏差还是耐心，取决于交易的功能。投机者持亏会扩大期货基差偏离并延缓其修正；套利者持亏获得更高收益，却不影响价格，价格修正速度取决于套利资本与保证金制度。

摘要（英文）

Holding losing positions is usually treated as a bias that slows price adjustment, but in futures markets its consequences depend on the function of the trade. We combine account-level data from a Chinese futures brokerage with a trade-level agent-based model of daily settlement, margin calls, and forced liquidation, calibrated to the basis of CSI 300 index futures. In the accounts, the disposition effect is concentrated in a reluctance to realize deepening losses, and margin tied up in losers crowds out new positions. In the model, speculators who hold losers keep stale exposure that widens the basis and slows its correction, and the effect rises steeply with the strength of the bias. Arbitrageurs who hold losers earn more than those who cut losses, and those who bank winners early earn less, yet neither half of their bias moves prices: arbitrage capital and the margin system set the speed of correction. Lower margin speeds correction but, under separate margining, more than doubles forced liquidation, a cost that portfolio margining removes. At the empirical distribution of the bias, its aggregate effect on the mean basis deviation is about 5%.

Do Managers Underreact? Subjective Uncertainty and the Revision of Range Earnings Forecasts

工作论文

业绩预告的区间宽度直接度量管理层自身的不确定性。管理层对信息反应不足，主观不确定性较高时也没有出现更强的过度反应，与近期一类预期形成理论的预测不同。

摘要（英文）

Theories of belief formation disagree on whether expectations underreact or overreact to news, and a recent class of models predicts that overreaction strengthens when subjective uncertainty is high. We test this prediction using the range earnings forecasts that Chinese listed firms issue before the end of a reporting period. The width of a manager’s forecast range provides a direct, firm-level measure of the manager’s own uncertainty at the time of the forecast. Using 20,391 firm-period forecast sequences from 2010 to 2025, we regress the next revision of expectations on the initial forecast revision and its interaction with range width. Managers underreact: an upward initial revision predicts a further upward revision, and the effect is four times larger when the next information point is the manager’s own follow-up forecast. Subjective uncertainty does not amplify overreaction. The interaction is close to zero, precisely estimated, and indistinguishable from a simulated benchmark that captures how firms steer reported earnings into their own forecast ranges. Range width does carry information about uncertainty: it rises with recent earnings volatility, and outcome dispersion rises with width. As earlier Chinese evidence suggests, realized earnings cluster just above the lower bound of the range, the opposite of the upper-bound tilt documented for U.S. firms.

以旧换新补贴的效率与公平——基于中国家庭金融调查的证据

工作论文

利用中国家庭金融调查 2025 年数据发现，近八成以旧换新补贴流向了没有补贴也会发生的购买，每 1 元补贴约对应 1.05 元原本不会发生的购买，远低于按销售额计算的约 5.9 元；领取环节的门槛使老年、农村家庭领取较少。

摘要

消费品以旧换新是近年来提振消费的重要政策，但官方统计的“带动销售额”并不等同于补贴创造的新增消费。本文利用中国家庭金融调查2025年数据中家庭对补贴领取、补贴金额以及“若没有补贴当年是否仍会购买”的直接回答，从效率与公平两个维度评估2024—2025年家电与数码产品补贴。研究发现：第一，按补贴金额计算，近八成补贴流向了没有补贴也会在当年发生的购买；每1元补贴约对应1.05元原本不会发生的购买，扣除补贴本身后约为0.84元，远低于按销售额计算的约5.9元。由此反推的隐含需求价格弹性约为1.3，高比例的“意外之财”源于普惠式补贴覆盖了全部购买者，而非补贴没有改变购买行为。第二，补贴率越高，家庭越可能报告补贴改变了购买决策，自报反事实呈现与理论预测相符的剂量反应。第三，补贴绝对额偏向高收入家庭，但相对于收入呈累进分布；高低收入家庭补贴差距的近一半来自购买之后的领取环节，老年、农村和不使用移动支付的家庭领取率较低。第四，收入最高的家庭领取补贴最多，其购买受补贴的影响却最小；流动性受约束的家庭更可能因补贴而购买，领取率却低约11个百分点。研究表明，降低领取门槛并向流动性受约束和中低收入家庭倾斜，有望同时提升补贴的效率与公平。

住房空间、购买能力与家庭耐用品配置升级——基于中国家庭金融调查的证据

与张金林合作·工作论文

利用中国家庭金融调查数据发现，住房面积与耐用品存量价值的正向关系随收入提高而增强。基础家电普及以后，收入增长能否转化为耐用品配置升级，与住房空间密切相关。

摘要

收入增长和耐用品以旧换新补贴能够提高家庭购置耐用品的能力，却不会直接扩大设备摆放和使用所需的住房空间。本文使用2021年、2023年和2025年中国家庭金融调查数据，以城镇产权自住家庭为样本，考察住房面积、家庭收入与厨卫大件、大件家电和空气调节与处理设备配置之间的关系。研究发现，住房面积与耐用品存量价值之间的正向关系随家庭收入提高而增强，收入第90百分位家庭的面积边际效应约为第20百分位家庭的1.67倍。按样本均值换算，住房面积每高出10%，收入第20和第90百分位家庭的耐用品存量价值分别高出约1.7%和2.9%。这一关系集中体现在已拥有相应设备家庭的配置水平上，在家庭是否拥有相应设备上没有稳定体现。独立厨房和独立卫生间与厨卫大件的配置价值相联系，房间数与空气调节设备的配置价值相联系，一线及新一线城市中的关系更强。研究表明，在基础家电普及以后，收入增长能否转化为耐用品配置升级，与住房空间密切相关。

系统性金融风险监测预警方法的演进与比较——兼论中国情境下的适用边界

工作论文

梳理系统性金融风险监测预警方法，区分危机概率识别与脆弱性监测两类任务，讨论机器学习和文本方法在其中的作用，以及这些方法在中国情境下的适用边界。

摘要

系统性金融风险监测预警是宏观审慎监管的重要内容。本文在梳理国内外文献的基础上，将相关方法区分为两类任务：一类是以信号提取法和多元选择模型为代表的危机概率识别，另一类是以信贷缺口、债务偿付比率、金融周期、债务可持续性分析、投入产出分析和网络分析为代表的脆弱性监测与结构分析。树模型、循环神经网络、文本方法和在线学习等数据驱动方法，主要在前述两类任务中扩展非线性识别、时序建模、文本信息提取和模型组合能力。总体来看，不同方法在识别对象、时间窗口、数据条件和解释能力上各有侧重，尚不存在适用于所有场景的统一预警框架。对中国而言，更可行的做法是结合房地产、地方政府债务、结构变化和中文文本信息等现实特征，把危机识别、脆弱性监测与数据驱动方法放在同一框架下相互印证。
